# Sales-Hr-finance-analysis
## Objective
Analyzed three business datasets (Sales, HR, Finance) using Excel 
to support operational and performance reporting through interactive dashboards.

## Tools Used
Excel, Pivot Tables, Power Query, Power Pivot, Slicers

## Sales Dashboard
- Built an interactive dashboard with slicers for City, Product Category, 
  Region, and Sale Date (2023-2024)
- Visualized total sales by product category (pie chart), by city (bar chart), 
  and sales trend over time (line chart)
- Used pivot tables to break down sales by region, city, and product category 
  to identify top and underperforming segments

## HR Dashboard
- Built a dashboard tracking Total Employees, Active Employees, Attrition Rate, 
  Average Salary, Average Age, and Average Satisfaction Score as key metrics
- Added slicers for Department, Attrition, Gender, and Hire Date to filter 
  workforce data dynamically
- Used pivot tables to analyze average salary by job title and performance 
  rating, and employee counts by department, city, and hire year
- Identified attrition patterns across departments to support retention planning

## Finance Dashboard
- Tracked Total Revenue, Total Expense, Net Profit, Profit Margin, Total 
  Budgeted Amount, and Budget Variance as core financial KPIs
- Built pivot tables comparing actual amount vs. budgeted amount by department 
  and category (e.g. Marketing Spend, Office Supplies, Salaries, Rent)
- Visualized expense distribution by department (pie chart) and budget vs. 
  actual variance (bar chart)
- Added Department, Category, and Date slicers for dynamic financial reporting

## Key Insights
- HR: Attrition is spread fairly evenly across departments, with average 
  salary varying by job title and performance rating
- Finance: Budget variance analysis highlights where actual spend diverges 
  from planned budgets by department
- Sales: Clear seasonal trends visible in the monthly sales line chart, 
  with performance varying significantly by city and product category
